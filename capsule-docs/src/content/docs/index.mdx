## What sets Capsule apart

<FeatureGrid>
  <Feature icon="random" title="Organized at any scale" href="/features/smart-albums/" linkLabel="Smart albums">
    Search, smart albums and automatic tagging are designed to run on your devices, over
    a full local index, so a library of millions of photos stays navigable without
    anyone sorting it by hand.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="padlock" title="A server with no keys" href="/design/cryptography/" linkLabel="Cryptography design">
    The server stores ciphertext and never holds a key. It cannot read a photo, a caption
    or an album title, yet it signs a custody receipt for every upload it accepts.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="server" title="Sharing across servers" href="/design/federation/" linkLabel="Federation design">
    Trust lives in the cryptography rather than the server, so people on different
    Capsule servers are meant to share albums with each other, with no central service
    in between.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="laptop" title="Your whole library, offline" href="/features/offline-library/" linkLabel="Offline library">
    Every native app is built to hold a full index of every photo you have kept, so all
    of it can be browsed, searched and organized with no connection.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="approve-check-circle" title="Verifiable history" href="/design/cryptography/provenance/" linkLabel="Provenance design">
    Every asset carries a signed, append-only provenance chain, so which person and
    device imported or edited it is verified, not taken on trust.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon="database" title="Recoverable by construction" href="/design/backup-recovery/" linkLabel="Backup and recovery">
    Each asset is paired with a signed metadata sidecar, so a library stays readable
    without a database and can be rebuilt from partial damage.
  </Feature>
</FeatureGrid>

## Everything you would expect

Originals kept at full quality and restorable byte for byte. RAW and video as
first-class formats. Native apps built with each platform's own controls. A
[CLI and TUI](/reference/cli/) that can do everything the apps can.

## Why it is built this way

Most photo services make you choose between the features a server gets by reading your
library and the privacy you get when it cannot. Capsule takes the privacy and rebuilds
the features on your devices, over data only they can decrypt.

That choice has costs, and the design docs state them rather than hiding them: what a
key-free server [can and cannot tell you](/design/notifications/), what happens
[when a key is lost](/design/backup-recovery/#recovery-paths), and what a faulty or hostile
client [is prevented from doing](/design/threat-model/).
